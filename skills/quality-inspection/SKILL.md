# Quality Inspection

## Purpose
Inspect and summarize manufacturing measurement data.

## Inputs
Numeric measurement list.

## Processing
Validate types and finite values, then compute count, mean, median, spread, minimum, and maximum.

## Outputs
Structured measurement summary.

## Limitations
Does not determine product conformity without specification limits.

## Expected Behavior
The skill validates inputs before calculation, returns structured results, and reports failures rather than fabricating values.
