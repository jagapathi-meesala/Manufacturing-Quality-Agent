# Process Capability

## Purpose
Calculate Cp/Cpk from measurements and specification limits.

## Inputs
At least two measurements plus lower and upper specification limits.

## Processing
Compute mean, sigma, Cp, Cpu, Cpl, and Cpk.

## Outputs
Capability metrics with specification context.

## Limitations
Capability interpretation depends on the applicable engineering specification and sampling assumptions.

## Expected Behavior
The skill validates inputs before calculation, returns structured results, and reports failures rather than fabricating values.
